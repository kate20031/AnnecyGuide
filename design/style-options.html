Lac & Sommets

Style B

Signalétique campus

Inspired by transit signs and wayfinding. Each topic has its own color, bold condensed headings, a departure board for bus lines. Very fast to scan, very practical.

Style C

Carnet savoyard

Friendly and student-made, like a travel notebook. Savoy-flag red, sticky-note tips, highlighted words. Feels written by students, for students.
